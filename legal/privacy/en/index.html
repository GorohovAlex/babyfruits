Privacy Policy — Baby Fruit

Last updated: 2026-10-26

Baby Fruit (“the app”) is published by Horokhov Oleksandr (“we”, “us”), Ukraine. Contact: 2gocomua@gmail.com.

The short version: your pregnancy data stays on your device (and, if you turn it on, in your own iCloud). We do not have accounts or servers and we never receive your data. The app shows no ads and uses no third-party analytics or tracking.

1. What the app stores

You enter, and the app keeps on your device:

  • your first name (or any name you choose);
  • the date and method used to calculate the pregnancy (last period, conception, or IVF transfer date and embryo age);
  • medications you add: name, dose, schedule, reminder settings and “taken” marks;
  • hCG test results you add: date, value and day;
  • the app language.

This is health-related information. It is stored in a file in the app’s private storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS data protection. We do not receive, see or store it anywhere else.

2. iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on sync in Settings, the same data is copied to your iCloud key-value storage so it appears on your other devices signed in with the same Apple Account. It is processed by Apple under your agreement with Apple and under Apple’s privacy policy. We cannot access your iCloud. Turning sync off removes the copy from iCloud.

3. Apple Watch and widgets

The app shares the data it needs (current week, medications for today) with the Apple Watch app and with the Home Screen widgets. This happens on your own devices only: through Apple’s WatchConnectivity and a shared app container.

4. Reminders

Medication reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Their text can contain the medication name. They are not sent through any server.

5. Backups and sharing

“Save a copy” writes a file only where you choose to save it. The share button creates an image of the week card and passes it to the app you choose. We do not see either.

6. Purchases

The app is free and has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

7. What we do not do

We do not show ads, do not use analytics or any tracking, do not create profiles, do not sell data and do not run our own servers that hold your data.

8. Retention and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. “Settings → Your data → Delete all data” removes the local data, scheduled reminders and the iCloud copy. Deleting the app removes the local data; a copy left in iCloud can be removed in the app before deleting it, or in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage storage.

9. Your rights

Because we do not receive your data, we cannot look it up or change it on our side; you can view, correct, export and delete everything inside the app. For any privacy question, or to exercise rights under the GDPR or other local law (access, correction, deletion, objection, complaint to a supervisory authority), contact 2gocomua@gmail.com.

10. Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children’s data.

11. Medical disclaimer

Baby Fruit is for general information and entertainment. Fruit sizes, weights, facts, event dates and any calculated due date are approximate. The app does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, does not interpret test results (including hCG), does not prescribe or change medications or doses, and does not replace a visit to your doctor. Always follow your doctor’s guidance; in an emergency, contact your local emergency services.
