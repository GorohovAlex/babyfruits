Privacy Policy — Baby Fruit

Last updated: 2026-11-27

Baby Fruit (“the app”) is published by Horokhov Oleksandr (“we”, “us”), Ukraine. Contact: 2gocomua@gmail.com.

The short version: your pregnancy data stays on your device (and, if you turn it on, in your own iCloud). We do not have accounts or servers and we never receive your data. The only third party that processes information in the free version is Google AdMob, which shows the ad banner.

1. What the app stores

You enter, and the app keeps on your device:

  • your first name (or any name you choose);
  • the date and method used to calculate the pregnancy (last period, conception, or IVF transfer date and embryo age);
  • medications you add: name, dose, schedule, reminder settings and “taken” marks;
  • hCG test results you add: date, value and day;
  • test records you add (date, name, clinic) and the files you attach (photos, scans or PDFs, including pages scanned with the camera). Camera access is used only when you choose to scan a document; photos and scans stay in the app’s storage;
  • belly photos you take (the “Belly photos” screen): the camera is used only while that screen is open, and photos are kept in the app’s private storage on your iPhone, labeled with the date and week. They are not synced through iCloud and are not included in the backup file you export. A photo is copied to your Photos library only when you tap “Save to Photos” (the app asks for add-only access, and cannot read your other photos);
  • the app language.

This is health-related information. It is stored in a file in the app’s private storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS data protection. We do not receive, see or store it anywhere else.

You enter this information yourself and it is processed only on your device, at your own choice. You can stop at any time by deleting it in the app (see section 9).

2. iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on sync in Settings, the same data is copied to your iCloud key-value storage so it appears on your other devices signed in with the same Apple Account. It is processed by Apple under your agreement with Apple and under Apple’s privacy policy. We cannot access your iCloud. Turning sync off removes the copy from iCloud.

If you turn on “Store files in iCloud” in Settings, attached files and their details are kept in the app’s iCloud Drive container of your own Apple Account, not visible to us. Turning it off or deleting all data removes them from there.

3. Apple Watch and widgets

The app shares the data it needs (current week, medications for today) with the Apple Watch app and with the Home Screen widgets. This happens on your own devices only: through Apple’s WatchConnectivity and a shared app container.

4. Reminders

Medication reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Their text can contain the medication name. They are not sent through any server.

If you turn on the optional “Fact of the day” notification, the app schedules a short local notification every day at 10:00 that says a new fact is ready. It does not contain any of your data. The setting is stored only on your device and can be turned off at any time in the app.

5. Backups and sharing

“Save a copy” writes a file only where you choose to save it. The share button creates an image of the week card and passes it to the app you choose. We do not see either.

6. Purchases

The app is free to download. Optional Pro features can be bought as a subscription or a one-time purchase through Apple. Payment is handled entirely by Apple under your agreement with Apple. We do not receive your payment details, name or Apple Account; the app only learns, on your device, whether Pro is active. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

7. Ads (free version)

The free version shows a banner ad provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process, on its own responsibility: device information, IP address, ad interactions and, only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt, the advertising identifier (IDFA). If you refuse, ads are not personalized. In the EEA, the UK and some other regions Google’s consent form asks for your choice; you can change it later in Settings → “Ad privacy options”.

  • We never pass the data described in section 1 to Google or to any advertiser.
  • We block sensitive ad categories (for example health, medicines) in AdMob.
  • Baby Fruit Pro removes ads.
  • More: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

8. What we do not do

We do not use analytics, do not create profiles, do not sell data and do not run our own servers that hold your data.

If you choose to share crash or usage reports with app developers in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may pass us anonymous diagnostic reports. They do not contain the data you enter in the app.

9. Retention and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. “Settings → Your data → Delete all data” removes the local data (including belly photos), scheduled reminders and the iCloud copy. Deleting the app removes the local data; a copy left in iCloud can be removed in the app before deleting it, or in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage storage.

10. Your rights

Because we do not receive your data, we cannot look it up or change it on our side; you can view, correct, export and delete everything inside the app. For any privacy question, or to exercise rights under the GDPR or other local law (access, correction, deletion, objection, complaint to a supervisory authority), contact 2gocomua@gmail.com.

11. Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children’s data.

12. Medical disclaimer

Baby Fruit is for general information and entertainment. Fruit sizes, weights, facts, event dates and any calculated due date are approximate. The app does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, does not interpret test results (including hCG), does not prescribe or change medications or doses, and does not replace a visit to your doctor. Always follow your doctor’s guidance; in an emergency, contact your local emergency services.
